Living in McKinney

McKinney has a real historic downtown, plus everything from 1900s homes to brand-new master plans. It often gets you more house than Frisco or Allen.

Home typesSingle-family, Historic homes, New build, Townhome
Home ageHistoric core (early 1900s) through active new construction to the north and west
LotsVaried: historic lots downtown, master-planned lots, acreage at the edges
HOAHOAs common in newer communities; historic district has preservation rules instead
School districtsMcKinney ISD, Frisco ISD (parts), Prosper ISD (parts), Lovejoy ISD (parts)

Getting around

Craig Ranch and the SH 121 side reach Legacy and Frisco easily. The north and east sides mean longer drives to Dallas.

  • Downtown Dallas ~37 min 31 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~20 min 15 mi
  • DFW Airport ~46 min 36 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~65 min 57 mi

Strengths

  • Distinctive historic downtown
  • Wide range of price points and home ages
  • Often more space per dollar than Frisco

Tradeoffs

  • Longer commute to Dallas from the north side
  • US 380 traffic
  • MUD/PID taxes in some new communities

Nearby

Historic Downtown McKinney square, Craig Ranch, Towne Lake Park, Erwin Park

Development watch

Steady growth along SH 121 and US 380. Watch planned roadway projects on US 380 for commute impact.
