Uptown & Victory Park (Dallas)

Uptown is Dallas's densest walkable district, with condo towers, restaurant-lined McKinney Avenue and the Katy Trail. Victory Park adds the arena, the Perot Museum and newer high-rises on the downtown edge.

Strengths

  • Walkable dining and nightlife
  • Fast access to Downtown
  • Streetcar, rail and trail options

Tradeoffs

  • HOA dues can be high
  • Little private outdoor space
  • Event and weekend traffic and parking

Knox-Henderson & Oak Lawn (Dallas)

Knox-Henderson pairs a shopping and dining strip with the Katy Trail on either side of US 75. Oak Lawn, to the west, is a dense, central mix of condos, townhomes and the Cedar Springs dining corridor.

Strengths

  • Katy Trail access
  • Dense dining and shopping
  • Very central location

Tradeoffs

  • Premium pricing for small lots
  • Construction and traffic on Knox and Henderson
  • Weekend parking
