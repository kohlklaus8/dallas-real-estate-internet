How to read this market

Months of inventory

Active listings divided by monthly sales: how long today's supply would last with no new listings. It's the single best read on who has leverage.

Days on market & sale-to-list

Fast sales near or above list price mean little room to negotiate. Longer days and lower ratios mean concessions, repairs and rate buy-downs are on the table.

Rates vs. price

A one-point move in mortgage rates changes your payment more than a modest price cut. Builders often use rate buy-downs, so compare the payment, not just the price.
