Living in Design District & Medical District / Love Field, Dallas

The Design District grew from warehouses into showrooms, galleries, restaurants and apartments along the Trinity levee. Beyond it, the Southwestern Medical District and Love Field form a major job and travel hub.

Home typesLuxury apartment, Condo / loft, Townhome, Single-family (Love Field area)
Home ageDesign District housing is mostly 2010s to present; neighborhoods near Love Field are largely 1930s to 1960s single-family homes.
LotsUrban infill sites in the Design District; standard city lots in the residential pockets near Love Field.
HOACondos and townhomes carry HOAs; older single-family areas near Love Field generally do not.
School districtsDallas ISD

Getting around

Downtown is minutes away on I-35E or DART Green/Orange, which also serve the Medical District and Love Field. North job centers are reached via the Tollway or I-35E.

  • Downtown Dallas ~9 min 4 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~28 min 20 mi
  • DFW Airport ~25 min 17 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~38 min 33 mi

Strengths

  • Close to Downtown and the hospitals
  • DART and TRE access
  • Near Love Field

Tradeoffs

  • Airport and freeway noise in places
  • Mostly rental and condo housing in the core
  • Limited grocery and daily retail in pockets

Nearby

Dallas Market Center, Trinity Strand Trail, Dallas Love Field, Frontiers of Flight Museum, UT Southwestern Medical Center, Parkland Memorial Hospital

Development watch

Children's Health and UT Southwestern broke ground in 2024 on a $5 billion pediatric campus at Harry Hines and Mockingbird. Love Field expects to start a terminal and concourse expansion in 2027, and Hi Line Square is planned in the Design District. Verify current timelines.
