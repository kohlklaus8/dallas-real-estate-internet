Living in Near Southside & Magnolia, Fort Worth

The Near Southside mixes Fort Worth's hospital district with Magnolia Avenue's restaurants and bars. Fairmount, one of the Southwest's largest historic districts, holds most of the older homes.

Home typesHistoric single-family, Bungalow / Victorian, Townhome, Mid-rise apartment
Home ageLate-1800s to 1920s homes in Fairmount, plus apartment and townhome infill from the 2010s to present.
LotsStandard older city lots with alleys; infill sites are small and dense.
HOAMost single-family homes have no HOA; the Fairmount historic district sets exterior design rules.
School districtsFort Worth ISD

Getting around

Downtown Fort Worth is just north, and T&P Station (TRE and TEXRail) is nearby. Dallas is a 45-minute-plus drive or a TRE ride; Legacy and Frisco are far.

  • Downtown Dallas ~43 min 33 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~54 min 45 mi
  • DFW Airport ~38 min 26 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~6 min 2 mi

Strengths

  • Walkable Magnolia dining
  • Historic Fairmount homes
  • Close to Downtown and the hospitals

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes need upkeep
  • Parking on Magnolia
  • Long commute to Dallas

Nearby

Magnolia Avenue, Fairmount–Southside Historic District, Cook Children's Medical Center, JPS Health Network, Fort Worth Medical District

Development watch

Trinity Metro plans to extend TEXRail 2.1 miles from T&P Station to a Medical District station, with groundbreaking targeted for late 2026 and opening around 2029. UT Southwestern broke ground on a cancer center in the Near Southside in 2025.
