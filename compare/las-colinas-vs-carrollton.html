Las Colinas (Irving)

A master-planned business district with canals, lakes and lock-and-leave living between Dallas and the airport.

Strengths

  • Central between Dallas and the airport
  • Walkable canal district
  • Lots of corporate jobs nearby

Tradeoffs

  • Fewer traditional single-family streets
  • HOA dues common
  • Can feel quiet after hours

Carrollton

Practical, well-connected and less expensive than the Collin County new-build belt, with a rail-served downtown square.

Strengths

  • Highway and rail access
  • Strong dining variety
  • Value relative to Collin County

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes
  • Three counties mean varied tax rates
  • Some areas near busy corridors
