Living in Lower Greenville & M Streets, Dallas

Lower Greenville is a long-running entertainment strip, anchored by the 1946 Granada Theater. Next to it, the M Streets are known for blocks of 1920s-30s Tudor cottages.

Home typesTudor cottage, Craftsman bungalow, Duplex / fourplex, Apartment / mid-rise
Home ageThe M Streets (Greenland Hills) were mostly built in the 1920s and 1930s; newer mid-rise apartments line Greenville Avenue.
LotsNarrow, standard city lots with alleys and mature trees; little room for large yards.
HOANo mandatory HOA on most streets; the M Streets conservation district sets rules for exterior changes and new builds.
School districtsDallas ISD

Getting around

Downtown and Uptown are a short drive down US 75 or Ross Avenue, and Mockingbird Station is close by for DART. US 75 is the route north to Richardson and Plano.

  • Downtown Dallas ~9 min 3 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~31 min 23 mi
  • DFW Airport ~35 min 23 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~48 min 35 mi
