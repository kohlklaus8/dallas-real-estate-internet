Living in Frisco

Frisco is the default answer for many relocating buyers: newer homes, big-league sports and entertainment, and a deep bench of master-planned communities with amenity centers.

Home typesSingle-family, Townhome, Luxury new build, Condo (limited)
Home ageMostly 1995–present; active new construction on the north side
LotsStandard suburban lots in master-planned communities; larger lots in select enclaves
HOAHOAs are the norm, often with amenity centers and pools
School districtsFrisco ISD, Prosper ISD (parts), Lewisville ISD (parts), Little Elm ISD (parts)

Getting around

Strong for Legacy/Plano and Frisco job centers. Downtown Dallas is a Tollway drive that is longer at peak hours.

  • Downtown Dallas ~35 min 27 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~10 min 6 mi
  • DFW Airport ~37 min 26 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~57 min 47 mi

Strengths

  • Wide selection of newer homes
  • Entertainment and dining density for a suburb
  • Close to the Legacy and SH 121 job corridors

Tradeoffs

  • Tollway traffic and toll costs to Dallas
  • MUD/PID districts can raise carrying costs
  • Pricing is competitive for move-up buyers

Nearby

The Star, Stonebriar Centre, Toyota Stadium, Riders Field, Frisco Square, Hall Park

Development watch

Ongoing mixed-use growth along the Tollway and SH 121, including the PGA of America headquarters at Fields and Universal's family theme park project. Verify current timelines.
