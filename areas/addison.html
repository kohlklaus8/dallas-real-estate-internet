Living in Addison

A small town with a big restaurant scene. Lock-and-leave townhomes and condos sit minutes from the Tollway.

Home typesTownhome, Condo, Single-family (limited), Urban apartments
Home ageMostly 1980s–2000s, with newer townhome infill
LotsSmall lots; townhome and urban formats dominate
HOAMost townhomes and condos carry HOA dues
