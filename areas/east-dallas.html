Living in East Dallas & Lakewood/White Rock

East Dallas wraps around White Rock Lake, with tree-lined streets of Craftsman, Tudor and Spanish-style homes in Lakewood and grand houses on Swiss Avenue. The lake's trail loop and the Arboretum are the big draws.

Home typesSingle-family, Craftsman / Tudor, Mid-century ranch, Condo / duplex
Home ageMostly 1910s to 1950s in Lakewood and Old East Dallas, with ranch-era homes near the lake and scattered newer rebuilds.
LotsStandard to large city lots with mature trees; some larger lots near White Rock Lake and the Lakewood Country Club area.
HOAMost single-family streets have no mandatory HOA; city conservation and historic districts (including Swiss Avenue) set exterior rules in parts of the area.
School districtsDallas ISD

Getting around

Downtown is about a 10-15 minute drive via Gaston, Swiss or I-30, and DART Blue Line stations sit on the west and north sides. Reaching Plano and Frisco means US 75 traffic.

  • Downtown Dallas ~11 min 5 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~33 min 23 mi
  • DFW Airport ~39 min 24 mi

Strengths

  • White Rock Lake and trails
  • Architecturally varied older homes
  • Close-in location

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes may need updates
  • Prices are high for lot and home age
  • Lake-area traffic on weekends

Nearby

White Rock Lake Park, Dallas Arboretum and Botanical Garden, Lakewood Theater, Swiss Avenue Historic District, Santa Fe Trail, Lakewood Country Club

Development watch

Mostly infill, including townhome projects along Gaston Avenue and teardown-rebuilds on older lots. Verify current projects.
