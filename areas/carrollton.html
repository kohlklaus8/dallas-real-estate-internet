Living in Carrollton

Practical, well-connected and less expensive than the Collin County new-build belt, with a rail-served downtown square.

Home typesSingle-family, Townhome, Urban near downtown station
Home ageMostly 1970s–1990s; newer infill near Downtown Carrollton
LotsStandard suburban lots
HOAMany older neighborhoods have no mandatory HOA
School districtsCarrollton-Farmers Branch ISD, Lewisville ISD, Dallas ISD (parts)

Getting around

Multiple highways plus the DART Green Line, with Silver Line connections at Downtown Carrollton.

  • Downtown Dallas ~28 min 19 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~14 min 10 mi
  • DFW Airport ~25 min 16 mi
