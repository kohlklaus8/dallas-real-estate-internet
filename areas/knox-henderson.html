Living in Knox-Henderson & Oak Lawn, Dallas

Knox-Henderson pairs a shopping and dining strip with the Katy Trail on either side of US 75. Oak Lawn, to the west, is a dense, central mix of condos, townhomes and the Cedar Springs dining corridor.

Home typesTownhome, Mid-rise / high-rise condo, Luxury apartment, Older single-family (limited)
Home ageMix of 1920s-50s homes and duplexes, 1960s-80s condos, and many townhomes and towers from the 2000s to present.
LotsSmall urban lots; most new construction is townhomes or multifamily replacing older homes.
HOACondos and newer townhome rows usually have HOAs; older single-family streets typically do not.
School districtsDallas ISD

Getting around

Downtown and Uptown are a few minutes away by car, Katy Trail or DART. The Tollway and US 75 run north to Plano and Frisco, with peak congestion.

  • Downtown Dallas ~7 min 3 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~28 min 20 mi
  • DFW Airport ~31 min 20 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~44 min 36 mi

Strengths

  • Katy Trail access
  • Dense dining and shopping
  • Very central location

Tradeoffs

  • Premium pricing for small lots
  • Construction and traffic on Knox and Henderson
  • Weekend parking

Nearby

Katy Trail, Knox Street shops, Henderson Avenue, Turtle Creek Park, Reverchon Park, Katy Trail Ice House

Development watch

The Knox, a roughly 1-million-square-foot project next to the Katy Trail, includes a 27-story apartment tower, a 140-room hotel with condos and an office building. The hotel and office are set for late 2026.
