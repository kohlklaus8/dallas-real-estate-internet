Living in TCU & Berkeley, Fort Worth

The TCU area mixes the campus and its game-day energy with Berkeley Place's 1920s-50s homes on tree-lined streets. Forest Park, the zoo and Trinity Trails are close by.

Home typesSingle-family, Tudor / Craftsman, Townhome, Condo / rental near campus
Home ageMostly 1920s to 1950s homes in Berkeley Place and around TCU, with townhome and multifamily infill near campus.
LotsStandard to large city lots with mature trees; smaller infill lots near University Drive and Berry Street.
HOAMost single-family streets have no HOA; newer townhomes and condos usually do.
School districtsFort Worth ISD

Getting around

Downtown Fort Worth is about 10 minutes north via University Drive or I-30. Dallas means an I-30 drive of 45 minutes or more, and north Dallas job centers are far.

  • Downtown Dallas ~46 min 35 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~58 min 47 mi
  • DFW Airport ~41 min 29 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~9 min 4 mi

Strengths

  • Established tree-lined streets
  • Parks, zoo and trails nearby
  • Close to Downtown Fort Worth

Tradeoffs

  • Game-day traffic and parking
  • More rental housing close to campus
  • Long commute to Dallas

Nearby

TCU campus, Amon G. Carter Stadium, Fort Worth Zoo, Forest Park, Colonial Country Club, Trinity Trails

Development watch

TCU and Endeavor are building Morado on Berry, a 780-bed student housing project with 25,000 square feet of retail on West Berry Street, expected before the 2027-28 school year.
