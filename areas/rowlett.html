Living in Rowlett

Lake-adjacent homes at moderate prices, with a rail line into Dallas.

Home typesSingle-family, Lakefront, Townhome
Home ageMostly 1980s–2000s, with newer pockets
LotsStandard lots; many lake-adjacent streets
HOAMix; many older areas have no mandatory HOA
School districtsGarland ISD, Rockwall ISD (parts)

Getting around

The Bush Turnpike and I-30 reach Dallas and Richardson. The DART Blue Line ends at Downtown Rowlett.

  • Downtown Dallas ~27 min 20 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~32 min 26 mi
  • DFW Airport ~51 min 34 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~64 min 51 mi

Strengths

  • Lake access at moderate prices
  • DART rail at Downtown Rowlett
  • Bush Turnpike access

Tradeoffs

  • Older housing in many areas
  • Sapphire Bay's future is uncertain
  • Fewer big-retail options

Nearby

Lake Ray Hubbard, Downtown Rowlett, Paddle Point Park

Development watch

Sapphire Bay, a planned lakefront resort district, has housing built but its resort pieces remain unbuilt. The city issued a notice of default to the developer in January 2026.
