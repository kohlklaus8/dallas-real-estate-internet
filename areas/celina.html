Living in Celina

Celina is the far-north frontier: brand-new homes and big amenity communities, for buyers who accept today's drive.

Home typesSingle-family, New build, Acreage
Home ageOverwhelmingly new construction
LotsMaster-planned lots plus rural acreage at the edges
HOAMaster-planned HOAs (e.g., Light Farms, Mustang Lakes) with large amenity packages
School districtsCelina ISD, Prosper ISD (parts)

Getting around

Dallas North Tollway extensions are bringing it closer. Today it is a long drive to Dallas and a moderate one to Frisco.

  • Downtown Dallas ~53 min 41 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~28 min 20 mi
  • DFW Airport ~56 min 41 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~76 min 62 mi

Off-peak drive estimates from OpenStreetMap routing (OSRM), measured from each area's center on 2026-10-21. Rush hour is often much longer.
