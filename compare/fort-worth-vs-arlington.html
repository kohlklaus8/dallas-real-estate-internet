Fort Worth

A big city with a Western heart: museums, the Stockyards, a lively downtown and neighborhoods at every price point.

Strengths

  • Distinct culture and downtown
  • Wide range of prices
  • Strong arts scene

Tradeoffs

  • Far from Dallas job centers
  • Huge city with uneven neighborhoods
  • New suburbs can be far out

Arlington

Home of the Cowboys and Rangers: the entertainment capital of the metroplex, centrally placed between Dallas and Fort Worth.

Strengths

  • Entertainment District
  • Central between both cities
  • Moderate prices

Tradeoffs

  • Event-day traffic
  • Few rail options
  • Car-dependent
