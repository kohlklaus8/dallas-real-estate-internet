Choosing an electricity plan in DFW (without getting burned)

Most of DFW lets you pick your electricity provider. Here's how to read a plan before you sign.

Published 2026-10-15 · 4 min read · Source: Editorial (DREI). See PowerToChoose.org (Public Utility Commission of Texas).

Most of North Texas is in a deregulated electricity market. Your poles and wires belong to the utility (Oncor in most of DFW), but you choose the retail provider that bills you. Some cities and co-op areas are exceptions, so check your address first.

How to compare plans

  • Use PowerToChoose.org, the state's official comparison site.
  • Look at the price at your real usage, not just the advertised 1,000 kWh rate. Many plans use bill credits that only apply in a narrow usage band.
  • Read the Electricity Facts Label (EFL). It shows the true pricing, fees and contract length.
  • Check the early termination fee and what happens when the contract ends.

Tips for new homeowners

  • Ask the seller or your agent for a year of past usage so you can estimate your bills.
  • Fixed-rate plans are the most predictable through Texas summers.
  • Set a calendar reminder a month before your contract ends.
