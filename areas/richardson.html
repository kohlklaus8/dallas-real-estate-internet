Living in Richardson

Richardson offers mid-century homes on big leafy lots, great transit and quick access to Dallas, at prices below the Collin County new-build belt.

Home typesSingle-family (mid-century ranch), Townhome, Urban near CityLine
Home ageMostly 1950s–1980s, with newer infill and urban product at CityLine
LotsGenerous mid-century lots with mature trees
HOAMany neighborhoods have no mandatory HOA
School districtsRichardson ISD, Plano ISD (north Richardson)

Getting around

One of the best-connected suburbs. US 75, the Bush Turnpike and several DART stations reach Dallas and Plano.

  • Downtown Dallas ~19 min 14 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~22 min 16 mi
  • DFW Airport ~34 min 23 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~51 min 44 mi

Strengths

  • Excellent transit and highway access
  • Mid-century character and mature trees
  • More central than far-north suburbs

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes often need updates
  • Little new construction
  • Mixed ISD boundaries; verify by address

Nearby

CityLine, UT Dallas, Breckinridge Park, Canyon Creek, Galatyn Park

Development watch

Corporate campuses at CityLine and transit-oriented development around rail stations. The DART Silver Line adds east-west rail; confirm current service.
