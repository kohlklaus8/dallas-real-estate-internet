Living in Far North Dallas

Far North Dallas is one of the most central addresses for split commutes, with City of Dallas services and a wide mix of housing types.

Home typesSingle-family, Townhome, Condo
Home ageMostly 1970s–1990s
LotsStandard suburban lots; many townhome and condo communities
HOAVaries widely; condos and townhomes carry HOA dues
School districtsPlano ISD (parts), Richardson ISD (parts), Dallas ISD (parts)

Getting around

The Tollway gives a straight shot to Uptown and to Legacy, so it is central for two-job households.

  • Downtown Dallas ~21 min 15 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~13 min 9 mi
  • DFW Airport ~31 min 20 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~47 min 41 mi

Off-peak drive estimates from OpenStreetMap routing (OSRM), measured from each area's center on 2026-10-21. Rush hour is often much longer.

Strengths

  • Central between Uptown and Legacy
  • Wide price range including condos/townhomes
  • Established, close-in location

Tradeoffs

  • Older stock
  • ISD and county boundaries vary block to block
  • Tollway congestion

Nearby

Galleria Dallas (nearby), Campbell Road corridor dining, Bent Tree
