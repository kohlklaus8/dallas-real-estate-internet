MUDs and PIDs: the line item new-construction buyers miss

Two homes, same price, different monthly cost. Here's why, and how to check before you sign.

Published 2026-10-15 · 4 min read · Source: Editorial (DREI). Confirm district details for any specific property.

Across fast-growing North Texas, developers often fund roads, water and sewer through special districts. The two you'll see most:

  • MUD (Municipal Utility District): a taxing district that levies its own property tax rate on top of city, county and school district rates.
  • PID (Public Improvement District): an assessment, often a fixed annual installment, that pays for improvements in that specific community.

Why it matters

Special districts can add meaningfully to your annual bill. They affect your monthly payment, your affordability and, eventually, your resale comparison.

How to check

  • Ask for the full combined tax rate for the specific address.
  • Read the MUD/PID notices that come with the contract. Texas law requires certain disclosures.
  • Look up the property on the county appraisal district site.
