Living in Uptown & Victory Park, Dallas

Uptown is Dallas's densest walkable district, with condo towers, restaurant-lined McKinney Avenue and the Katy Trail. Victory Park adds the arena, the Perot Museum and newer high-rises on the downtown edge.

Home typesHigh-rise condo, Mid-rise condo, Townhome, Luxury apartment
Home ageMostly 1990s to present, with new towers still delivering and a few older homes in the State Thomas area.
LotsVery small or no private lots; most homes are condos or townhomes on tight urban sites.
HOACondo and townhome HOAs are nearly universal, and high-rise dues often cover concierge, amenities and building reserves.
School districtsDallas ISD

Getting around

Downtown Dallas is minutes away by car, the free M-Line streetcar or DART. The Tollway leads north toward Legacy and Frisco, which is a longer drive at peak hours.

  • Downtown Dallas ~5 min 2 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~28 min 21 mi
  • DFW Airport ~29 min 20 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~42 min 35 mi

Strengths

  • Fast access to Downtown
  • Streetcar, rail and trail options

Tradeoffs

  • HOA dues can be high
  • Little private outdoor space
  • Event and weekend traffic and parking
