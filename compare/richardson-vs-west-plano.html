Richardson

Richardson offers mid-century homes on big leafy lots, great transit and quick access to Dallas, at prices below the Collin County new-build belt.

Strengths

  • Excellent transit and highway access
  • Mid-century character and mature trees
  • More central than far-north suburbs

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes often need updates
  • Little new construction
  • Mixed ISD boundaries; verify by address

West Plano

Established, centrally located and close to big employers. You get mature neighborhoods without a far-north commute.

Strengths

  • Central to North Dallas job centers
  • Mature trees and established neighborhoods
  • Walkable pockets at Legacy

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes may need updating
  • Limited new construction
  • Competitive pricing near Legacy
