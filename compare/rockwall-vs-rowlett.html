Rockwall

Lakeside living east of Dallas, with The Harbor's waterfront dining and a small-town square.

Strengths

  • Lake views and waterfront dining
  • Newer homes
  • Small-town downtown

Tradeoffs

  • I-30 bridge traffic at peak hours
  • Far from the north job corridors
  • Prices higher than nearby Rowlett

Rowlett

Lake-adjacent homes at moderate prices, with a rail line into Dallas.

Strengths

  • Lake access at moderate prices
  • DART rail at Downtown Rowlett
  • Bush Turnpike access

Tradeoffs

  • Older housing in many areas
  • Sapphire Bay's future is uncertain
  • Fewer big-retail options
