Five small-town squares north of Dallas worth a Saturday

Historic downtowns with local restaurants, events and real character, minutes from the suburbs.

Published 2026-10-15 · 4 min read · Source: Editorial (DREI).

One of the best-kept secrets of North Texas suburbs is how many have a real historic downtown. If walkability and local character matter to you, these are worth a visit, and worth living near.

  • Historic Downtown McKinney: the region's best-known square, with locally owned shops and restaurants and a busy event calendar.
  • Downtown Plano: a historic main street with DART rail access and Haggard Park.
  • Downtown Carrollton: a classic square served by the DART Green Line.
  • Celina's square: a small-town center in one of the fastest-growing areas in the region.
  • Historic Downtown Wylie: a compact, walkable main street near Lake Lavon.

Living near a square

Homes within walking distance of a historic downtown are limited and often older. That adds character, but sometimes means updates. Ask Dallas AI or a local expert which streets fit your budget.

Event calendars change. Check each city's official site before you go.
