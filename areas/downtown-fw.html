Living in Downtown Fort Worth & Sundance Square

Downtown Fort Worth centers on Sundance Square's 35 blocks of plaza, dining and entertainment, plus Bass Hall. The Texas A&M campus and convention center work are adding activity.

Home typesLoft condo, High-rise condo, Luxury apartment, Townhome (limited)
Home ageHistoric early-1900s buildings converted to lofts, plus condos and apartments from the 1990s to present.
LotsNo private lots to speak of; housing is condos, lofts and apartments.
HOACondo HOAs are standard, with dues tied to building amenities and age.
School districtsFort Worth ISD

Getting around

TRE runs to Downtown Dallas and TEXRail runs to DFW Airport, and Trinity Metro's free downtown Blue Line circulates locally. Driving to Dallas is 45 minutes or more on I-30.

Strengths

  • Walkable plaza and performing arts
  • TRE and TEXRail access
  • Lower pricing than central Dallas towers

Tradeoffs

  • Limited condo inventory
  • Garage parking costs
  • Long drive to Dallas job centers

Nearby

Sundance Square Plaza, Bass Performance Hall, Fort Worth Water Gardens, Fort Worth Convention Center, Sid Richardson Museum, Trinity Trails

Development watch

Texas A&M-Fort Worth opened its Law and Education Building in August 2026 and broke ground on an 11-story research building due in 2029. The convention center is in a phased expansion.
