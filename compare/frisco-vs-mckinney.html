Frisco

Frisco is the default answer for many relocating buyers: newer homes, big-league sports and entertainment, and a deep bench of master-planned communities with amenity centers.

Strengths

  • Wide selection of newer homes
  • Entertainment and dining density for a suburb
  • Close to the Legacy and SH 121 job corridors

Tradeoffs

  • Tollway traffic and toll costs to Dallas
  • MUD/PID districts can raise carrying costs
  • Pricing is competitive for move-up buyers

McKinney

McKinney has a real historic downtown, plus everything from 1900s homes to brand-new master plans. It often gets you more house than Frisco or Allen.

Strengths

  • Distinctive historic downtown
  • Wide range of price points and home ages
  • Often more space per dollar than Frisco

Tradeoffs

  • Longer commute to Dallas from the north side
  • US 380 traffic
  • MUD/PID taxes in some new communities
