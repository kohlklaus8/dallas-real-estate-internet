Getting around

Next to DFW Airport. SH 114 and SH 121 reach Dallas, Las Colinas and Fort Worth. TEXRail runs from Grapevine Main to Fort Worth, and the DART Silver Line serves the airport area.

  • Downtown Dallas ~29 min 23 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~28 min 20 mi
  • DFW Airport ~15 min 7 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~30 min 23 mi
