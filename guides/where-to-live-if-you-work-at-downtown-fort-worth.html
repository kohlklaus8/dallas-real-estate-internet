Where to live if you work in downtown Fort Worth

Every DFW area we cover, sorted by drive time to downtown Fort Worth, with typical home values and school districts.

Published 2026-10-22 · 5 min read · Source: Drive times: OpenStreetMap routing (OSRM), off-peak, measured 2026-10-21. Home values: Zillow Home Value Index by ZIP, September 2026. School districts: Dallas Real Estate Internet area profiles.

Downtown Fort Worth and Sundance Square anchor the west side of the metro, with the Cultural District and Near Southside a few minutes away. Below, every area we cover is sorted by its off-peak drive time to downtown Fort Worth, with typical home values and school districts so you can weigh the trade-offs at a glance.

Drive times are off-peak estimates from each area's center (OpenStreetMap routing, measured October 2026). Rush hour is often much longer, so test-drive your commute at the time you'd actually make it.

About 20 minutes or less

  • Fort Worth: you're already there. Typical home values $184K to $686K (by ZIP). Schools: Fort Worth ISD, Keller ISD (parts).
  • Downtown Fort Worth & Sundance Square: you're already there. Typical home values $267K (by ZIP). Schools: Fort Worth ISD.
  • West 7th & Cultural District / Arlington Heights: about 5 min (2 mi). Typical home values $361K (by ZIP). Schools: Fort Worth ISD.
  • Near Southside & Magnolia: about 6 min (2 mi). Typical home values $184K to $209K (by ZIP). Schools: Fort Worth ISD.
  • TCU & Berkeley: about 9 min (4 mi). Typical home values $209K to $686K (by ZIP). Schools: Fort Worth ISD.

20 to 35 minutes

  • Arlington: about 23 min (15 mi). Typical home values $230K to $380K (by ZIP). Schools: Arlington ISD, Mansfield ISD (parts).
  • Grapevine: about 30 min (23 mi). Typical home values $545K (by ZIP). Schools: Grapevine-Colleyville ISD.

35 to 45 minutes

  • Las Colinas: about 36 min (29 mi). Typical home values $455K to $632K (by ZIP). Schools: Irving ISD, Carrollton-Farmers Branch ISD (parts).
  • Design District & Medical District / Love Field: about 38 min (33 mi). Typical home values $272K to $1.07M (by ZIP). Schools: Dallas ISD.
  • Oak Cliff & Bishop Arts: about 41 min (31 mi). Typical home values $199K to $411K (by ZIP). Schools: Dallas ISD.
  • Deep Ellum & Downtown Dallas: about 41 min (33 mi). Typical home values $262K to $972K (by ZIP). Schools: Dallas ISD.
  • Flower Mound: about 42 min (34 mi). Typical home values $525K to $761K (by ZIP). Schools: Lewisville ISD, Argyle ISD (parts).
  • Uptown & Victory Park: about 42 min (35 mi). Typical home values $415K to $972K (by ZIP). Schools: Dallas ISD.
  • Carrollton: about 43 min (36 mi). Typical home values $343K to $516K (by ZIP). Schools: Carrollton-Farmers Branch ISD, Lewisville ISD.
  • Knox-Henderson & Oak Lawn: about 44 min (36 mi). Typical home values $415K to $2.13M (by ZIP). Schools: Dallas ISD.

How to narrow it down

  • Decide your real limit first. A 25-minute off-peak drive can be 40 minutes at 8 a.m.
  • Check the toll roads on your route. The Dallas North Tollway, President George Bush Turnpike and SH 121 save time but add up over a year (see our Toll roads 101 guide).
  • Price the whole payment, not just the house. Property tax rates differ by city and school district, and some newer neighborhoods add MUD or PID taxes.
  • School districts follow addresses, not city lines. Verify the exact school zone for any home you like.
