Living in Preston Hollow, Dallas

Preston Hollow means estate lots, luxury new builds and a short drive to everything in central Dallas.

Home typesEstate single-family, Luxury new build, Teardown/rebuild lots
Home ageMid-century originals alongside extensive new luxury construction
LotsLarge lots, including some of Dallas's biggest in-city estates
HOAMostly no mandatory HOA
School districtsDallas ISD

Getting around

Minutes to Uptown, Downtown and Love Field. One of the shortest luxury commutes in the region.

  • Downtown Dallas ~11 min 7 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~21 min 17 mi
  • DFW Airport ~32 min 24 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~46 min 39 mi

Off-peak drive estimates from OpenStreetMap routing (OSRM), measured from each area's center on 2026-10-21. Rush hour is often much longer.
