Allen

Allen is a polished, mid-sized suburb with 1990s–2000s homes, strong retail and a central position on US 75.

Strengths

  • Central US 75 location
  • Good retail and parks
  • Mostly established, move-in-ready homes

Tradeoffs

  • Limited new-build land
  • US 75 peak congestion
  • Competitive at popular price points

West Plano

Established, centrally located and close to big employers. You get mature neighborhoods without a far-north commute.

Strengths

  • Central to North Dallas job centers
  • Mature trees and established neighborhoods
  • Walkable pockets at Legacy

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes may need updating
  • Limited new construction
  • Competitive pricing near Legacy
