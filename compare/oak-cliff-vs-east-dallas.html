Oak Cliff & Bishop Arts (Dallas)

Oak Cliff was a separate town until Dallas annexed it in 1903 and keeps its own feel, with historic districts, hills and parks. Bishop Arts is its walkable core of independent shops, restaurants and galleries.

Strengths

  • Historic homes with character
  • Independent dining and shopping
  • Close to Downtown

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes may need updating
  • Busy streets and parking around Bishop Arts
  • Housing stock varies block to block

East Dallas & Lakewood/White Rock

East Dallas wraps around White Rock Lake, with tree-lined streets of Craftsman, Tudor and Spanish-style homes in Lakewood and grand houses on Swiss Avenue. The lake's trail loop and the Arboretum are the big draws.

Strengths

  • White Rock Lake and trails
  • Architecturally varied older homes
  • Close-in location

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes may need updates
  • Prices are high for lot and home age
  • Lake-area traffic on weekends
