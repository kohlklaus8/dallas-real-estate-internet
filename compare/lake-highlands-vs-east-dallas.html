Lake Highlands (Dallas)

Lake Highlands is a postwar neighborhood of ranch homes on tree-lined streets, just north and east of White Rock Lake. It sits within Dallas city limits but is served mostly by Richardson ISD.

Strengths

  • Larger lots inside Dallas
  • Close to White Rock Lake trails
  • DART Blue Line access

Tradeoffs

  • Many 1960s-70s homes need updating
  • Car-oriented layout
  • Mix of school districts by address

East Dallas & Lakewood/White Rock

East Dallas wraps around White Rock Lake, with tree-lined streets of Craftsman, Tudor and Spanish-style homes in Lakewood and grand houses on Swiss Avenue. The lake's trail loop and the Arboretum are the big draws.

Strengths

  • White Rock Lake and trails
  • Architecturally varied older homes
  • Close-in location

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes may need updates
  • Prices are high for lot and home age
  • Lake-area traffic on weekends
