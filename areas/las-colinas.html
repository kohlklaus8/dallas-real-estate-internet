Living in Las Colinas, Irving

A master-planned business district with canals, lakes and lock-and-leave living between Dallas and the airport.

Home typesCondo, Townhome, Single-family, Urban apartments
Home age1970s master plan to present, with lots of newer urban product
LotsSmall to standard lots; many condo and townhome options
HOAMost condos and townhomes have HOA dues
School districtsIrving ISD, Carrollton-Farmers Branch ISD (parts), Coppell ISD (parts)

Getting around

Central to DFW Airport, Dallas and the corporate offices in the Urban Center. DART's Orange Line serves Las Colinas.

  • Downtown Dallas ~19 min 13 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~28 min 22 mi
  • DFW Airport ~19 min 11 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~36 min 29 mi
