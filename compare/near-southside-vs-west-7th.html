Near Southside & Magnolia (Fort Worth)

The Near Southside mixes Fort Worth's hospital district with Magnolia Avenue's restaurants and bars. Fairmount, one of the Southwest's largest historic districts, holds most of the older homes.

Strengths

  • Walkable Magnolia dining
  • Historic Fairmount homes
  • Close to Downtown and the hospitals

Tradeoffs

  • Older homes need upkeep
  • Parking on Magnolia
  • Long commute to Dallas

West 7th & Cultural District / Arlington Heights (Fort Worth)

The Cultural District holds Fort Worth's major museums, Dickies Arena and the Will Rogers complex. West 7th adds dense dining and apartments, and Arlington Heights offers older homes on brick-paved Camp Bowie.

Strengths

  • World-class museums and arena
  • Dining near West 7th
  • Close to Downtown Fort Worth

Tradeoffs

  • Event traffic around Dickies Arena
  • Older Arlington Heights homes need updates
  • Long commute to Dallas
