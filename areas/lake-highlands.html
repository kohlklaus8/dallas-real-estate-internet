Living in Lake Highlands, Dallas

Lake Highlands is a postwar neighborhood of ranch homes on tree-lined streets, just north and east of White Rock Lake. It sits within Dallas city limits but is served mostly by Richardson ISD.

Home typesSingle-family ranch, Updated / rebuilt home, Townhome, Condo / apartment
Home ageMostly 1950s to 1970s ranch-style homes, with remodels and some newer builds.
LotsGenerous suburban-style lots by Dallas standards, many with mature trees and alleys.
HOAMost single-family areas have no mandatory HOA; voluntary neighborhood associations are common, and condos and townhomes carry HOA dues.
School districtsRichardson ISD, Dallas ISD (parts)

Getting around

DART Blue Line stations (White Rock, Lake Highlands, LBJ/Skillman) run to Downtown, and LBJ and US 75 connect to Richardson and Plano job centers.

  • Downtown Dallas ~18 min 10 mi
  • Legacy West (Plano) ~25 min 19 mi
  • DFW Airport ~36 min 25 mi
  • Downtown Fort Worth ~53 min 45 mi

Tradeoffs
