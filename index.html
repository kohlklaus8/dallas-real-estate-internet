Why DFW

Why people are moving to Dallas–Fort Worth

More reasons →
$

No state income tax

Texas has no personal state income tax, so more of each paycheck stays with you. Property taxes do more of the work, and we explain how.

↗

A major job hub

Corporate headquarters, finance, tech, healthcare and logistics, many along the Legacy, Frisco and Las Colinas corridors.

✈

Two major airports

DFW International and Dallas Love Field put most of the country within a few hours' flight.

★

Big-league everything

The Cowboys, Rangers, Mavericks, Stars and FC Dallas, plus concerts and events nearly every weekend.

♨

Food & small-town squares

Tex-Mex, barbecue and everything else, plus historic downtowns in McKinney, Plano and Carrollton.

⌂

Room to grow

New communities, established neighborhoods and acreage, with more home for the money than many big metros.
