Living in West 7th & Cultural District / Arlington Heights, Fort Worth

The Cultural District holds Fort Worth's major museums, Dickies Arena and the Will Rogers complex. West 7th adds dense dining and apartments, and Arlington Heights offers older homes on brick-paved Camp Bowie.

Home typesSingle-family, Townhome, Luxury apartment, Condo
Home ageArlington Heights homes are mostly 1920s to 1940s; West 7th townhomes and apartments are mostly 2010s to present.
LotsStandard older lots in Arlington Heights; small, dense sites around West 7th.
HOAArlington Heights single-family streets generally have no HOA; West 7th townhomes and condos usually do.
School districtsFort Worth ISD

Getting around

Downtown Fort Worth is a few minutes east on West 7th Street or I-30. Dallas is a 45-minute-plus I-30 drive.

Development watch

The $1.7 billion Westside Village broke ground in March 2026 on the former Fort Worth ISD headquarters site at University Drive. Its first phase includes an office building and 308 homes. The eight-story Van Zandt project is underway at West 7th and Foch.
